# Article 11a - Quote size below which the pre-trade transparency requirements under Articles 14, 15, 16 and 17 of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 apply

The obligation to make public firm quotes in respect of shares, depositary receipts, ETFs, certificates, and other similar financial instruments shall apply to *[[2017R0587_EN.1#(4)|systematic internalisers]]* when they deal in sizes up to twice the standard market size as determined in accordance with [[2017R0587_EN.11|Article 11]].
