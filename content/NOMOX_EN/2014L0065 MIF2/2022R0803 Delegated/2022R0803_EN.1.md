# Article 1 - Scope


This Delegated Regulation applies to ‘data reporting services providers’, or ‘DRSPs’, as defined in [[2014R0600_EN.2#1#36a|Article 2(1)(36a) of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]], that are subject to ESMA supervision.
