# Article 33 - SME growth markets

## 1.
Member States shall provide that the operator of an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* may apply to its home *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* to have the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, registered as an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*.
## 2.
Member States shall provide that the home *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* may register the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, as an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* if the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* receives an application as referred to in paragraph 1 and is satisfied that the conditions set out in paragraph 3 are complied with in relation to the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or that the conditions in paragraph 3a are complied with in relation to a segment of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*.
## 3.
Member States shall ensure that *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTFs]]* are subject to effective rules, systems and procedures which ensure that the following is complied with:
### (a)
at least 50 % of the issuers whose *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instruments]]* are admitted to trading on the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* are SMEs at the time when the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* is registered as an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* and in any calendar year thereafter;
### (b)
appropriate criteria are set for initial and ongoing admission to trading of *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instruments]]* of issuers on the market;
### (c)
on initial admission to trading of *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instruments]]* on the market there is sufficient information published to enable investors to make an informed judgment about whether or not to invest in the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instruments]]*, either an appropriate admission document or a prospectus if the requirements laid down in Directive 2003/71/EC are applicable in respect of a public offer being made in conjunction with the initial admission to trading of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instrument]]* on the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*;
### (d)
there is appropriate ongoing periodic financial reporting by or on behalf of an issuer on the market, for example audited annual reports;
### (e)
issuers on the market as defined in point (21) of [[2014R0596_EN.3#1|Article 3(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014, persons discharging managerial responsibilities as defined in point (25) of [[2014R0596_EN.3#1|Article 3(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 and persons closely associated with them as defined in point (26) of [[2014R0596_EN.3#1|Article 3(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 comply with relevant requirements applicable to them under [[2014R0596_EN.0|Regulation (EU) No 596/2014]];
### (f)
regulatory information concerning the issuers on the market is stored and disseminated to the public;
### (g)
there are effective systems and controls aiming to prevent and detect market abuse on that market as required under the [[2014R0596_EN.0|Regulation (EU) No 596/2014]].
## 3a.
Member States shall ensure that the relevant segment of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* is subject to effective rules, systems and procedures which ensure that the conditions set out in paragraph 3 and all of the following conditions have been complied with:
### (a)
the segment of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* registered as ‘*[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*’ is clearly separated from the other market segments operated by the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(1)|investment firm]]* or *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(18)|market operator]]* operating the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, which is, inter alia, indicated by a different name, different rulebook, different marketing strategy, and different publicity, as well as a specific allocation of the market identification code to the segment registered as *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* segment;
### (b)
the transactions made on the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* segment concerned are clearly distinguished from other market activity within the other segments of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*;
### (c)
upon the request of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(55)|home Member State]]* of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* shall provide a comprehensive list of the instruments listed on the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* segment concerned, as well as any information on the operation of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* segment that the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* may request.
## 4.
Compliance by the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(1)|investment firm]]* or *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(18)|market operator]]* operating the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, with the conditions laid down in paragraphs 3 and 3a is without prejudice to compliance by that *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(1)|investment firm]]* or *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(18)|market operator]]* with other obligations under this Directive relevant to the operation of *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTFs]]*. Without prejudice to paragraph 7, the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(1)|investment firm]]* or *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(18)|market operator]]* operating the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, may impose additional conditions.
## 5.
Member States shall provide that the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(55)|home Member State]]* of an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* may deregister an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, as an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* in any of the following cases:
### (a)
the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(1)|investment firm]]* or *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(18)|market operator]]* operating the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, applies for its deregistration;
### (b)
the conditions in paragraph 3 or 3a are no longer complied with in relation to the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof.
## 6.
Members States shall require that if a *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(26)|competent authority]]* of the *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(55)|home Member State]]* of an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]* registers or deregisters an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(22)|MTF]]*, or a segment thereof, as an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* under this Article, that authority shall as soon as possible notify ESMA of that registration or deregistration. ESMA shall publish on its website a list of *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth markets]]* and shall keep that list up to date.
## 7.
Member States shall require that where a *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instrument]]* of an issuer is admitted to trading on one *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*, that *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(15)|financial instrument]]* may also be traded on another *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(24)|trading venue]]* only where the issuer has been informed and has not objected. Where the other *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(24)|trading venue]]* is another *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]* or a segment of an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*, the issuer shall not be subject to any obligation relating to corporate governance, or initial, ongoing or ad hoc disclosure, with regard to that other *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*. Where the other *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(24)|trading venue]]* is not an *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth market]]*, the issuer shall be informed of any obligation to which the issuer will be subject that relates to corporate governance, or initial, ongoing or ad hoc disclosure, with regard to the other *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(24)|trading venue]]*. ESMA shall develop guidelines by 5 June 2026 with respect to the procedures for informing issuers and to the process for lodging objections, as well as the relevant timelines.
## 8.
The Commission is empowered to adopt delegated acts in accordance with [[2014L0065_EN.89|Article 89]] to supplement this Directive by further specifying the conditions laid down in paragraphs 3 and 3a of this Article. Those conditions shall take into account the need to maintain high levels of investor protection in order to promote investor confidence in those markets, while minimising the administrative burdens for issuers on the market. They shall also take into account that deregistrations are not to occur nor are registrations to be refused merely because of a temporary failure to comply with the condition laid down in paragraph 3, point (a), of this Article.
## 9.
The Commission shall set up an expert stakeholder *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(34)|group]]* by 1 July 2020 to monitor the functioning and success of *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(12)|SME growth markets]]*. By 1 July 2021, the expert stakeholder *[[2014L0065_EN.4#(34)|group]]* shall publish a report on its conclusions.
