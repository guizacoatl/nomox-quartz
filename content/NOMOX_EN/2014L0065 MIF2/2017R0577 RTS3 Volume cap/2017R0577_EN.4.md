# Article 4 - Format of the data requests

## 1.
Trading venues, APAs, and CTPs shall submit the data referred to in [[2017R0577_EN.2#1|Article 2(1)]] in a common XML format.
## 2.
Trading venues, APAs, and CTPs shall submit the data referred to in [[2017R0577_EN.2|Article 2]], (2) and (3), in the format specified in the request.
