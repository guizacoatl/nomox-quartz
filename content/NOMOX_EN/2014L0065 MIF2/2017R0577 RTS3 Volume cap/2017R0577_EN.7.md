# Article 7 - Reporting requirements for trading venues, APAs, and CTPs to ESMA for the trading obligation for derivatives


—————
## 2.
Trading venues, APAs, and CTPs shall, without undue delay and no later than three working days following the receipt of the data concerned, submit to ESMA the data for the determination of whether derivatives are sufficiently liquid as referred to in [[2017R0577_EN.1#1#h|Article 1(1), point (h)]].
