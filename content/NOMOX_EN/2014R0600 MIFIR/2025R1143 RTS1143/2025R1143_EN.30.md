# Article 30 - Information from joint applicants

In addition to the requirements set out in [[2025R1143_EN.15|Article 15]], joint applicants seeking authorisation to operate a CT shall include in their application for authorisation information on the necessity in terms of technical and logistical capacity for each applicant to jointly operate the CT.
