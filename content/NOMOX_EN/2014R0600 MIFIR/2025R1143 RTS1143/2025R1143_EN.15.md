# Article 15 - Information to ESMA

## 1.
An applicant seeking authorisation to operate a consolidated tape (‘CT’) pursuant to Article 27db of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 shall submit to ESMA the information set out in Articles [[2025R1143_EN.16|16]] to 29.
## 2.
A CTP shall promptly inform ESMA of any material change to the information provided at the time of the authorisation or thereafter.
