# Article 28 - Information on organisational requirements

An applicant seeking authorisation to operate a CT pursuant to Article 27db of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 shall provide ESMA with the information on the arrangements in place to ensure compliance with the organisational requirements laid down in [[2014R0600_EN.27h|Article 27h of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]].
