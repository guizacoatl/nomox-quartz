# Article 1 - Information to competent authorities

## 1.
An applicant seeking authorisation to operate an APA or an ARM pursuant to [[2014R0600_EN.27d|Article 27d of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]] shall submit to ESMA or, where relevant, the national competent authority the information set out in Articles [[2025R1143_EN.2|2]] to [[2025R1143_EN.7|7]], and the information regarding all the organisational requirements set out in Section II of this Chapter.
## 2.
An APA or ARM shall promptly inform ESMA or, where relevant, the national competent authority of any material change to the information provided at the time of the authorisation or thereafter.
