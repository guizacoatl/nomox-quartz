# Article 4a - Determining liquid markets for other similar financial instruments

For the purposes of [[2017R0567_EN.2#1#17|Article 2(1), point (17)]](b), of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014, other similar financial instruments shall be considered not to have a liquid market over their entire trading life.
