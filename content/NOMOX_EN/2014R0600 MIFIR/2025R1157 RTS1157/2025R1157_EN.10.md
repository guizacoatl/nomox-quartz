# Article 10 - Additional information to be provided by an applicant for authorisation as a CTP


At the request of ESMA, an applicant for authorisation as a CTP shall provide that authority with additional information that ESMA needs to proceed with the assessment of the application for authorisation within five working days following the receipt of ESMA’s request.
