# Article 9 - Acknowledgement of receipt of an application for authorisation as a CTP


Within 10 working days from the receipt of the application for authorisation, ESMA shall send electronically an acknowledgement of receipt to the applicant for authorisation as a CTP, including the contact details of the contact point designated pursuant to [[2025R1157_EN.7|Article 7]].
