# Article 12 - Communication of the decision to grant or refuse an authorisation to operate as a CTP


ESMA shall electronically inform an applicant for authorisation as a CTP of its decision to grant or to refuse such authorisation within five working days following the adoption of a reasoned decision as referred to in [[2025R1157_EN.27d|Article 27d]]b(3) of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014.
