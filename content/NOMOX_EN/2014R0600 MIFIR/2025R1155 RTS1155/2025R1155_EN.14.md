# Article 14 - Level of accuracy for DPEs

## 1.
DPEs shall record the date and time of reportable events up to one millisecond or better.
## 2.
DPEs shall ensure that their business clocks used to record the time of reportable events do not diverge by more than one millisecond from the reference time laid down in [[2025R1155_EN.11|Article 11]].
## 3.
By way of derogation from paragraphs 1 and 2, DPEs that have also acquired the status of systematic internaliser shall comply with [[2025R1155_EN.12|Article 12]].
