# Article 1 - Definitions


For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions shall apply:
### (a)
‘input data’ means data transmitted by data contributors to the CTP, in accordance with [[2014R0600_EN.22a#1|Article 22a(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014;
### (b)
‘output data’ means data disseminated by the CTP, in accordance with [[2014R0600_EN.27h#1#d|Article 27h(1), point (d), of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]].
