# Article 1 - Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions shall apply:
## (1)
‘*[[2017R0583_EN.1#(1)|central limit order book trading system]]*’ means any of the following:
### (a)
a continuous order book trading system that by means of an order book and a trading algorithm operated without human intervention matches sell orders with buy orders on the basis of the best available price on a continuous basis;
### (b)
a trading system combining elements of a continuous order book trading system, as referred to in point (a), and of a *[[2017R0583_EN.1#(2)|periodic auction trading system]]*, as defined in point (2);
## (2)
‘*[[2017R0583_EN.1#(2)|periodic auction trading system]]*’ means a trading system that matches orders on the basis of a periodic auction and a trading algorithm operated without human intervention.
