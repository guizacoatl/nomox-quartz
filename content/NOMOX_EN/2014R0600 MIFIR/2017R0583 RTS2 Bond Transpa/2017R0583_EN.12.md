# Article 12 - Application of post-trade transparency to certain transactions executed outside a trading venue

The obligations set out in [[2014R0600_EN.21#1|Article 21(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 shall not apply to transactions listed in [[2017R0590_EN.2#5|Article 2(5)]] of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/590.
