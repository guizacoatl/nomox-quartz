# Article 1a - Scope of application of Articles 3, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 13

## 1.
Articles [[2017R0583_EN.3|3]], [[2017R0583_EN.6|6]], [[2017R0583_EN.9|9]], [[2017R0583_EN.10|10]], [[2017R0583_EN.11|11]] and [[2017R0583_EN.13|13]] shall apply only in respect of derivatives. [[2017R0583_EN.8|Article 8]] shall apply only in respect of derivatives and package transactions.
## 2.
References to [[2014R0600_EN.11|Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]] in Articles [[2017R0583_EN.8|8]] and [[2017R0583_EN.11|11]] of this Regulation shall be construed as references to [[2014R0600_EN.11|Article 11 of Regulation (EU) No 600/2014]] as applicable before 28 March 2024.
