# Article 10a - Designated trading venues for the exchange of order data


The trading venues listed in Annex III shall be considered to be trading venues that have a significant cross-border dimension in the supervision of market abuse with respect to shares as referred to in [[2014R0596_EN.25a|Article 25a of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014]].
