# Article 14 - Attestation

## 1.
The attestation referred to in [[2022R2554_EN.26#7|Article 26(7)]] of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 shall contain the information set out in Annex VIII.
## 2.
Where several TLPT authorities have been involved in a TLPT, the lead TLPT authority shall provide the attestation referred to in [[2022R2554_EN.26#7|Article 26(7)]] of Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 to the tested financial entities.
