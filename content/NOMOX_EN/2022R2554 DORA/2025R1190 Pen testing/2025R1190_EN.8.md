# Article 8 - Specificities for pooled or joint TLPTs

## 1.
Unless otherwise decided by the lead TLPT authority, where several financial entities, identified in accordance with [[2025R1190_EN.16#2|Article 16(2)]] or (4), are involved in a pooled or joint TLPT, each financial entity shall follow each of the steps set out in Articles [[2025R1190_EN.9|9]] to [[2025R1190_EN.15|15]].
## 2.
Unless otherwise provided in this Regulation, where several TLPT authorities are involved in a joint TLPT or in a pooled TLPT, as referred to in [[2025R1190_EN.16#3|Article 16(3)]] or 16(5), references in Articles [[2025R1190_EN.9|9]] to [[2025R1190_EN.15|15]] to the ‘TLPT authority’ shall be understood as a reference to the lead TLPT authority for such pooled or joint TLPT.
