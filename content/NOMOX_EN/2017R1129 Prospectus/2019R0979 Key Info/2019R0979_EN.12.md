# Article 12 - Practical arrangements to ensure the machine readability of the data


The competent authority shall provide the accompanying data referred to in Articles [[2019R0979_EN.11|11]], [[2019R0979_EN.11a|11a]] and [[2019R0979_EN.11b|11b]] in a common XML format and in accordance with the format and standards set out in the tables in Annex VII.
