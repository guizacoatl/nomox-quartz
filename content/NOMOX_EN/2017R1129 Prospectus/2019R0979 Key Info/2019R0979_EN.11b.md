# Article 11b - Electronic copy of exemption documents


Competent authorities may provide ESMA via the notification portal referred to in [[2017R1129_EN.25#6|Article 25(6)]] of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 with an electronic copy of the documents referred to in [[2019R0979_EN.1#4|Article 1(4)]], points (da) and (db), and [[2019R0979_EN.1#5|Article 1(5)]], point (ba), of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 for the analysis referred to in [[2019R0979_EN.47#3#a|Article 47(3), point (a)]], of that Regulation.
