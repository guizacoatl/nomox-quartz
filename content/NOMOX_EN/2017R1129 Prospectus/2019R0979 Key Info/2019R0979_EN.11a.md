# Article 11a - Information accessible on European Single Access Point


For the purposes of [[2017R1129_EN.21a|Article 21a of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129]], competent authorities may make the information referred to in [[2019R0979_EN.21a#1|Article 21a(1)]] accessible on European Single Access Point (ESAP) by providing ESMA via the notification portal referred to in [[2019R0979_EN.25#6|Article 25(6)]] of that Regulation with an electronic copy of that information, and the data necessary for its classification in the storage mechanism referred to in [[2017R1129_EN.21#6|Article 21(6)]] of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129, in accordance with the tables set out in Annex VII to this Regulation.
