# Article 1 - Search for regulated information

## 1.
ESMA shall establish and operate the European Single Access Point (‘ESAP’) pursuant to [[2023R2859_EN.1#1|Article 1(1)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859 as the central access point for the search for regulated information at Union level by users.
## 2.
ESMA shall ensure that ESAP offers, regarding the regulated information made available by the officially appointed mechanisms (OAMs) referred to in Directive 2004/109/EC, the search criteria referred to in [[2023R2859_EN.7#3|Article 7(3)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859.
