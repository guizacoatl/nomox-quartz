# Article 5 - Common format for the delivery of metadata

## 1.
Each OAM shall deliver metadata to ESAP in the format specified in [[2025R1339_EN.5#2|Article 5(2)]] of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.
## 2.
Each OAM shall deliver metadata on regulated information to ESAP in accordance with the table set out in the Annex to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339.
