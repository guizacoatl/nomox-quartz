# Article 3 - Provision of information to ESAP by OAMs

## 1.
Each OAM shall provide to ESAP the regulated information referred to in [[2023R2859_EN.5#1#e|Article 5(1), point (e), of Regulation (EU) 2023/2859]] and within the time limits set out in [[2025R1339_EN.6|Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339]].
## 2.
OAMs shall provide to ESAP the metadata referred to in [[2025R1339_EN.5#1|Article 5(1)]] of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339, including all the metadata that issuers submit to the OAMs pursuant to [[2004L0109_EN.23a|Article 23a of Directive 2004/109/EC]].
## 3.
Each OAM shall make available to ESAP all language versions of the documents that are disseminated by issuers and stored by the OAM in accordance with [[2004L0109_EN.21#1|Article 21(1)]] of Directive 2004/109/EC.
## 4.
Where issuers rectify and resubmit information to an OAM, that OAM shall make available to ESAP the resubmitted document and the accompanying metadata within the time limits set out in [[2025R1339_EN.6|Article 6 of Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1339]].
## 5.
OAMs shall not charge ESMA for the delivery of regulated information, the metadata or, where required, the qualified electronic seal, nor for any cost the OAMs incur to connect to ESAP.
