# Article 60 - Disclosure of derogations


Where a Member State makes use of a derogation or option provided by [[2011L0061_EN.6|Article 6]] or 9, [[2011L0061_EN.15#4g|Article 15(4g)]] or [[2011L0061_EN.21|Article 21]], 22, 28 or 43, it shall inform the Commission thereof as well as of any subsequent changes. The Commission shall make the information public on a website or by other easily accessible means.
