# Article 1a - Format of the application


The application referred to in [[2018R1646_EN.1|Article 1]] shall be submitted by electronic means unless specified otherwise in the applicable national law. Those electronic means shall ensure the completeness, integrity and confidentiality of the information during its transmission. Each submitted document shall clearly identify to which specific requirement of this Regulation it refers.
