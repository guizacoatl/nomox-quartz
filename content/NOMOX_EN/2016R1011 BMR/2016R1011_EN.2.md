# Article 2 - Scope

## 1.
This Regulation applies to the provision of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(8)|contribution of input data]]* to a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* and the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(7)|use of a benchmark]]* within the Union.
## 1a.
Titles II, III, with the exception of Articles [[2016R1011_EN.23a|23a]], [[2016R1011_EN.23b|23b]] and [[2016R1011_EN.23c|23c]], IV, V and VI apply only in respect of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(25)|critical benchmarks]]*, *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(26)|significant benchmarks]]*, *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23a)|EU Climate Transition Benchmarks]]* and *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23b)|EU Paris-aligned Benchmarks]]*.
## 1b.
By way of derogation from paragraph 1a of this Article, [[2016R1011_EN.13#1#d|Article 13(1), point (d)]], and [[2016R1011_EN.27#2aa|Article 27(2aa)]] apply to all *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]* used in the Union provided by *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* that:
### (a)
are included in the register referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.36|Article 36]]; or
### (b)
belong to a group with at least one *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* included in the register referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.36|Article 36]].
## 1c.
By way of derogation from paragraph 1a of this Article, [[2016R1011_EN.19|Article 19]] applies to any *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23)|commodity benchmark]]* based on contributed *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(14)|input data]]*, unless any of the following conditions is fulfilled:
### (a)
it is a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(24)|regulated-data benchmark]]*;
### (b)
it is a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* based on submissions by *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(9)|contributors]]* the majority of which are supervised entities;
### (c)
it is a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(25)|critical benchmark]]* and the underlying asset is gold, silver or platinum.
## 2.
This Regulation shall not apply to:
### (a)
a central bank;
### (b)
a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(29)|public authority]]*, where it contributes data to, provides, or has control over the provision of, *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]* for public policy purposes, including measures of employment, economic activity, and inflation;
### (c)
a central counterparty (CCP), where it provides reference prices or settlement prices used for CCP risk-management purposes and settlement;
### (d)
the provision of a single reference price for any *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(16)|financial instrument]]* listed in Section C of Annex I to [[2014L0065_EN.0|Directive 2014/65/EU]];
### (e)
the press, other media and journalists where they merely publish or refer to a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* as part of their journalistic activities with no control over the provision of that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]*;
### (f)
a natural or legal person that grants or promises to grant credit in the course of that person's trade, business or profession, only insofar as that person publishes or makes available to the public that person's own variable or fixed borrowing rates set by internal decisions and applicable only to *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(18)|financial contracts]]* entered into by that person or by a company within the same group with their respective clients;
### (g)
a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23)|commodity benchmark]]* based on submissions from *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(9)|contributors]]* the majority of which are non-supervised entities and in respect of which the total average notional value of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(16)|financial instruments]]* referencing the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* does not exceed EUR 200 million over a period of 12 months;
### (h)
an *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(2)|index provider]]* in respect of an *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(1)|index]]* provided by said provider where that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(2)|index provider]]* is unaware and could not reasonably have been aware that that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(1)|index]]* is used for the purposes referred to in point (3) of [[2016R1011_EN.3#1|Article 3(1)]];
#### (i)
a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(22a)|spot foreign exchange benchmark]]* which has been designated by the Commission in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.18a#1|Article 18a(1)]].
