# Article 36 - Register of administrators and benchmarks

## 1.
ESMA shall establish and maintain a public register that contains the following information:
### (a)
the identities, including, where available, the legal entity identifier (LEI), of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* authorised or registered pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.34|Article 34]] and the competent authorities responsible for the supervision thereof;
### (b)
the identities, including, where available, the LEI, of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* that comply with the conditions laid down in [[2016R1011_EN.30#1|Article 30(1)]], the list of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their International Securities Identification Numbers (ISINs), referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.30#1#c|Article 30(1), point (c)]], and the third country competent authorities responsible for the supervision thereof;
### (c)
the identities, including, where available, the LEI, of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* that acquired recognition in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.32|Article 32]], the list of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, provided by those *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* which may be used in the Union and, where applicable, the third country competent authorities responsible for the supervision thereof;
### (d)
the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, that are endorsed in accordance with the procedure laid down in [[2016R1011_EN.33|Article 33]], the identities of their *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]*, and the identities, including, where available, the LEI, of the endorsing *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]*;
### (e)
the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, subject to a statement published by ESMA or a competent authority pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.24#2|Article 24(2)]], and hyperlinks to such statements;
### (f)
the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, subject to designations by competent authorities notified to ESMA pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.24#3|Article 24(3)]] or (7), and hyperlinks to such designations;
### (g)
the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, subject to designations by ESMA, and hyperlinks to such designations;
### (h)
the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, subject to public notices issued by ESMA and competent authorities pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.24a#6|Article 24a(6)]], and the hyperlinks to such public notices;
#### (i)
the list of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23a)|EU Climate Transition Benchmarks]]* and *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23b)|EU Paris-aligned Benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs, available for use in the Union;
### (j)
the list of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(25)|critical benchmarks]]*, including, where available, their ISINs;
### (k)
the list of *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(23)|commodity benchmarks]]* subject to Annex II available for use in the Union, including, where available, their ISINs.
## 2.
The register referred to in paragraph 1 shall be publicly accessible on the website of ESMA and shall be updated promptly, as necessary.
