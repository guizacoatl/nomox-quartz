# Article 24a - Requirements for administrators of significant benchmarks

## 1.
Within 60 working days of the notification referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#2|Article 24(2)]], the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* of a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* fulfilling the condition referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#1#a|Article 24(1), point (a)]], shall seek authorisation or registration with the competent authority of the Member State where it is *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(28)|located]]*. Where that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* is *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(28)|located]]* in a third country and unless the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* is covered by an equivalence decision adopted pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.30|Article 30]], that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* shall, within 60 working days of the notification referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#2|Article 24(2)]], seek either of the following:
### (a)
recognition with ESMA pursuant to the procedure set out in [[2016R1011_EN.32|Article 32]];
### (b)
endorsement pursuant to the procedure set out in [[2016R1011_EN.33|Article 33]], in which case the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* is to select an endorsing *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* in the Union that submits an application to ESMA.
## 2.
Within 60 working days of a designation as referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#3|Article 24(3)]], the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]*, unless that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* is already authorised or registered, shall seek authorisation or registration with the competent authority of the Member State where it is *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(28)|located]]* in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.34|Article 34]].
## 3.
Within 60 working days of a designation as referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#6|Article 24(6)]], the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]*, unless the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]* is covered by an equivalence decision adopted pursuant to [[2016R1011_EN.30|Article 30]], shall seek either of the following:
### (a)
recognition with ESMA pursuant to the procedure set out in [[2016R1011_EN.32|Article 32]];
### (b)
endorsement pursuant to the procedure set out in [[2016R1011_EN.33|Article 33]], in which case the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* is to select an endorsing *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* in the Union that submits an application to ESMA.
## 4.
Within 60 working days of a designation as referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#7|Article 24(7)]], the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]*, unless that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* is already authorised or registered, shall seek authorisation or registration with the designating competent authority in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.34|Article 34]].
## 5.
ESMA and the competent authorities shall make use of the supervisory and sanction powers they are entrusted with pursuant to this Regulation to ensure that the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrators]]* comply with their obligations.
## 6.
The competent authority or ESMA, as applicable, shall issue a public notice stating that a *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(26)|significant benchmark]]* provided by an *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* does not comply with this Regulation, and that users are to refrain from using that *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(3)|benchmark]]*, where any of the following conditions is fulfilled:
### (a)
within 60 working days of the notification referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#2|Article 24(2)]], of the designation referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#3|Article 24(3)]] or of the designation referred to in [[2016R1011_EN.24#6|Article 24(6)]], the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* concerned has not initiated procedures to comply with paragraph 1, 2 or 3 of this Article, respectively;
### (b)
the authorisation, registration, recognition or endorsement procedures have failed;
### (c)
ESMA has withdrawn the registration of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* concerned in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.31|Article 31]];
### (d)
ESMA has withdrawn or suspended the recognition of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* concerned in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.32#8|Article 32(8)]];
### (e)
the endorsement of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* concerned has ceased in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.33#6|Article 33(6)]];
### (f)
the competent authority has withdrawn or suspended the authorisation or registration of the *[[2016R1011_EN.3#(6)|administrator]]* concerned in accordance with [[2016R1011_EN.35|Article 35]].

Competent authorities shall notify ESMA of all issued public notices without undue delay. ESMA shall publish all issued public notices on its website. ESMA and the competent authority shall remove the public notice without undue delay as soon as the reason for which it was issued is no longer valid.
