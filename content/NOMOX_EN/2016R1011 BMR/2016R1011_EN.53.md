# Article 53 - ESMA reviews


—————
## 2.
ESMA shall have the power to require the documented evidence from a competent authority for any of the decisions adopted in accordance with the first subparagraph of [[2016R1011_EN.51#2|Article 51(2)]] and [[2016R1011_EN.25#2|Article 25(2)]], as well as for actions taken with regard to the enforcement of [[2016R1011_EN.24#1|Article 24(1)]].
