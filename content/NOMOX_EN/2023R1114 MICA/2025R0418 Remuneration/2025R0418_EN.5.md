# Article 5 - Identification of staff members

## 1.
Issuers of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens, shall identify all *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* members that have a material impact on the risk profile of those issuers or on the risk profile of the tokens they issue, by applying at least the criteria set out in paragraphs 2 and 3.
## 2.
A person shall be identified as a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member referred to in paragraph 1 if it meets one or more of the following criteria:
### (a)
they are members of the management body or *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(2)|senior management]]*;
### (b)
they have *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(4)|managerial responsibility]]* over the issuer’s *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control functions]]* or *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(7)|material business units]]*;
### (c)
they have *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(4)|managerial responsibility]]* for:
#### (i)
management of at least one of the following risk categories: liquidity risk, operational risk, including legal risk and information and communication technology risk;
#### (ii)
information and communication technology used for the processing of the tokens;
#### (iii)
the prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing;
#### (iv)
the management of reserve assets;
#### (v)
the token issuance function;
#### (vi)
managing outsourcing arrangements with third-party service providers supporting critical or important functions;
#### (vii)
finance, including taxation and budgeting;
#### (viii)
legal affairs;
#### (ix)
the soundness of accounting policies and procedures;
#### (x)
human resources;
#### (xi)
the establishment or internal approval of white papers.
## 3.
A person shall be considered a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member referred to in paragraph 1 where its professional activities have a comparable impact on the issuers’ risk profile or on the risk profile of the tokens they issue to that of the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* members specified in points (a) to (c) of paragraph 2.
