# Article 4 - Remuneration policies for all staff

## 1.
Issuers of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens shall ensure that their *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policies for all *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* meet the following criteria:
### (a)
they are consistent with the rights and interests of holders of tokens with a view to ensuring that holders are treated fairly and that their interests are not impaired by the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* practices adopted by the issuer;
### (b)
they are gender-neutral and based on the principle of equal pay for male and female *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* for equal work or work of equal value within the meaning of [[2013L0036_EN.3#1#65|Article 3(1), point (65), of Directive 2013/36/EU]];
### (c)
they are consistent with the objectives of the business and risk strategy, including environmental, social and governance (ESG) risk-related objectives, corporate culture and values, risk culture and *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(5)|risk appetite]]*;
### (d)
they ensure that the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* engaged in *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control functions]]* are remunerated in accordance with the achievement of the objectives linked to their functions and independently of the performance of the business areas they control;
### (e)
they are consistent with the management of ESG risks and provide for incentives for the control and limitation of ESG impacts caused by the issuer’s business activities;
### (f)
they do not create a conflict of interest or incentive that may lead *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* members to favour their own interests or the issuer’s interests to the potential detriment of any holder of significant tokens they issue;
### (g)
they do not encourage risk-taking that exceeds the level of *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(5)|risk appetite]]* of the issuer;
### (h)
they are available to the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* concerned at all times;
#### (i)
they are transparent to all *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* regarding the fixed *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, processes and criteria for setting the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* and the award criteria used;
### (j)
they are clear, well documented, transparent, proportionate to the size, internal organisation and nature, as well as to the scope and complexity of the issuer’s business activities.
