# Article 3 - Governance arrangements for remuneration policies

## 1.
The management body of issuers of asset referenced tokens and e-money tokens shall carry out all the following tasks:
### (a)
approve and retain ultimate responsibility for the issuer’s *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policy;
### (b)
approve any changes to the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policy;
### (c)
seek advice from the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* committee where established by the issuer, on the issuer’s *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policy.
## 2.
Issuers of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens shall ensure the following:
### (a)
the implementation of their *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policies is subject to a review for compliance with policies and procedures by *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control functions]]* at least annually;
### (b)
the compliance function and the risk management function, where established, or *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* entrusted with the performance of compliance procedures or risk management procedures, the internal audit function, where established, and human resources function provide effective input on the design of the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policies;
### (c)
potential conflicts of interest caused by the pay-out in instruments as part of the variable or fixed *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* are identified and appropriately mitigated.
## 3.
The review referred to in point (a) of paragraph (2) may be outsourced to an external party.
