# Article 2 - Definitions


For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions shall apply:
## (1)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|Staff]]*’ means all employees of an issuer of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens, and all members of such issuer’s management bodies;
## (2)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(2)|Senior management]]*’ means those natural persons who exercise executive functions within an issuer of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens and who are responsible and accountable to the management body, but are not members of that body, for the day-to-day management of the issuer under the direction of its management body;
## (3)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(3)|Identified staff]]*’ means *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* that has a material impact on the risk profile of the issuer of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens, or on the risk profile of significant asset-referenced or electronic-money tokens they issue;
## (4)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(4)|Managerial responsibility]]*’ means a situation, in which a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member either:
### (a)
leads a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business unit]]*, *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(7)|material business unit]]* or a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control function]]* and is directly accountable to the management body as a whole or to a member of the management body or to the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(2)|senior management]]*; or
### (b)
carries out a function or a task laid down in [[2025R0418_EN.5#2#c|Article 5(2), point (c)]];
## (5)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(5)|Risk appetite]]*’ means the aggregate level and types of risk that an issuer of asset-referenced tokens or e-money token is willing to assume within its risk capacity, in line with its business model, to achieve its strategic objectives;
## (6)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|Business unit]]*’ means a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business unit]]* as defined in [[2013R0575_EN.142#1#3|Article 142(1), point (3), of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013]];
## (7)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(7)|Material business unit]]*’ means a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business unit]]* as defined in [[2013R0575_EN.142#1#3|Article 142(1), point (3), of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013]] that is assessed by the issuer of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens as having a material impact on the issuer’s business model or as representing a material source of revenue, profit or franchise value for an issuer;
## (8)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|Control function]]*’ means a function that is independent from the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business units]]* it controls and that is responsible for internal control procedures and includes the risk management, compliance and the internal audit functions;
## (9)
‘*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|Remuneration]]*’ means all forms of fixed and variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, including the following:
### (a)
monetary or non-monetary payments and benefits, awarded directly to *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* by or on behalf of issuers of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens in exchange for professional services provided by *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]*;
### (b)
carried interest payments within the meaning of [[2011L0061_EN.4#1#d|Article 4(1), point (d), of Directive 2011/61/EU]];
### (c)
other payments made via methods and vehicles which, if they were not considered as *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, would lead to a circumvention of the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* requirements set out in Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 and in this Regulation.
