# Article 6 - Remuneration policies for identified staff members

## 1.
Issuers of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens shall ensure that the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policies for *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* identified in accordance with [[2025R0418_EN.5|Article 5 of this Regulation]], taking into account national contract and labour law, make a clear distinction between the following two components of the total *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*:
### (a)
basic fixed *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, which shall primarily reflect relevant professional experience and organisational responsibility as set out in a *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member’s job description as part of the terms of employment;
### (b)
variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* which shall reflect a sustainable and risk adjusted performance as well as performance in excess of that required to fulfil the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member’s job description as part of the terms of employment.
## 2.
Issuers of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens shall ensure that their *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policies for *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* identified in accordance with [[2025R0418_EN.5|Article 5 of this Regulation]] comply with the following requirements:
### (a)
variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* is linked to the assessment of the performance of the issuer, the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business unit]]* and the individual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member concerned, and, when assessing performance, financial and non-financial criteria, including the management of ESG risks and control over adverse ESG impacts, are taken into account;
### (b)
existence of an effective risk adjustment mechanism to integrate all relevant types of current and future risks;
### (c)
absence of guaranteed variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* other than for new *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* only for the first year of employment;
### (d)
*[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* packages relating to compensation or buy out from contracts in previous employment are aligned with the long-term interests of the issuer;
### (e)
payments relating to the early termination of an employment contract reflect performance achieved over time by the individual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member and do not reward failure or misconduct;
### (f)
fixed and variable components of total *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* are appropriately balanced, and the fixed component represents a sufficiently high proportion of the total *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* to allow the operation of fully flexible policies on variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* components, including the possibility of reducing the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* to zero;
### (g)
appropriate maximum ratios are set between the variable and the fixed component of the total *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, taking into account the business activities of the issuer and associated risks, as well as the impact that different categories of *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* referred to in [[2025R0418_EN.5|Article 5]] have on the risk profile of the issuers or on the risk profile of the tokens they issue;
### (h)
variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* for *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* in *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control function]]* is predominantly linked to control objectives and that the ratio between the variable and the fixed components of total *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* for *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* in *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(8)|control functions]]* is set significantly lower compared to the ratio applicable to the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business units]]* they control;
#### (i)
at least 50 % of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* consists of any of the following instruments:
#### (i)
shares or equivalent ownership interests, subject to the legal structure of the issuer concerned;
#### (ii)
share-linked instruments, subject to the legal structure of the issuer concerned;
#### (iii)
Additional Tier 1 instruments which can be fully converted to Common Equity Tier 1 instruments or written down and which adequately reflect the credit quality of the issuer as a going concern;
#### (iv)
asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens issued by the issuer, unless the issuer is a credit institution, investment firm, UCITS management company or Alternative Investment Fonds Managers (AIFM) and is required to pay out a part of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* of *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* in instruments in accordance with [[2013L0036_EN.94#1#l|Article 94(1), point (l), of Directive 2013/36/EU]], [[2019L2034_EN.32#1#j|Article 32(1), point (j), of Directive (EU) 2019/2034]] or [[2009L0065_EN.14b#1#m|Article 14b(1)(m) of Directive 2009/65/EC]] or paragraph 1(m) of Annex II to [[2011L0061_EN.0|Directive 2011/61/EU]];
#### (v)
other instruments that may be used for the pay-out of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* by the issuer, if the issuer is authorised in accordance with a Union legal act that requires the issuer to pay out parts of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* in such other instruments;
### (j)
specific criteria are set for the application of malus and clawback on variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*, which shall in particular cover situations where the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member concerned:
#### (i)
participated in or was responsible for conduct which resulted in significant losses for the issuer, as defined in the issuer’s *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* policy;
#### (ii)
failed to meet appropriate standards of fitness and propriety;
### (k)
at least 40 % of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* awarded to *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(3)|identified staff]]* is deferred for a period of at least 3 to 5 years, depending on the business cycle of the issuer, the nature of its business, its risks and the activities of the individual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member concerned, except in the case of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* of a particularly high amount where the proportion of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* deferred is at least 60 %;
### (l)
the deferred portion of the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* referred to in point (k) does not vest sooner than 12 months after the start of the deferral period and does vest no faster than on a pro-rata basis;
### (m)
no interest or dividend on instruments which have been awarded as variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* under deferral arrangements is paid to *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(3)|identified staff]]* for periods before the instrument has vested;
### (n)
the variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* is awarded and vests only if it is sustainable according to the financial situation of the issuer as a whole and justified on the basis of the performance of the issuer, of the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(6)|business unit]]* and of the *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member concerned;
### (o)
no obligation is created to pay variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* during the period when the issuer failed to meet prudential requirements set out in accordance with [[2023R1114_EN.67|Article 67 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114]].
## 3.
The requirement in paragraph 2, point (i) shall apply to the deferred and the non-deferred part of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*. Where the issuer of asset referenced tokens or e-money tokens pays out a higher portion than 50 % of the deferred part of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* in instruments referred to in paragraph 2, point (i) it may pay out a lower portion than 50 % of the non-deferred part of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* in instruments referred to in paragraph 2, point (i), as long as in total the requirement for the pay out of variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* in instruments of at least 50 % is met.
## 4.
Paragraph 2, points (i) and (k), shall not apply to an individual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member whose annual variable *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]* does not exceed EUR 50 000 and does not represent more than one fourth of that individual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(1)|staff]]* member’s total annual *[[2025R0418_EN.2#(9)|remuneration]]*.
