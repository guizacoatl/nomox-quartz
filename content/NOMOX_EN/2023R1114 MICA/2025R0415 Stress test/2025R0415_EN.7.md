# Article 7 - Minimum frequency of the different stress testing exercises

## 1.
The frequency for solvency stress test shall be, at least, quarterly for issuers of significant asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens and semi-annual for issuers of non-significant asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens.
## 2.
The frequency of liquidity stress test shall be, at least, monthly for all issuers of asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens.
