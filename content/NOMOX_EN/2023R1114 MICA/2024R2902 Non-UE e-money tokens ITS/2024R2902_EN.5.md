# Article 5 - Retention period of personal data by issuers


The issuers shall not retain any personal data on holders submitted by the crypto-asset service providers in accordance with Articles [[2024R2902_EN.1#2|1(2)]] and [[2024R2902_EN.3#2|3(2)]] of this Regulation for longer than necessary to comply with the reporting obligations laid down in [[2023R1114_EN.22#1|Article 22(1)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Such retention period shall not exceed 5 years from the date of obtaining the personal data by the issuers.
