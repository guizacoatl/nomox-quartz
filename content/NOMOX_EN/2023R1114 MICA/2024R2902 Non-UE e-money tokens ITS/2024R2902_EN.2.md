# Article 2 - Reporting reference dates

## 1.
For the purposes of the reporting requirement referred to in [[2023R1114_EN.22#1|Article 22(1)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, issuers shall submit information to competent authorities on a quarterly reporting basis, with the following reporting reference dates: 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December.
## 2.
The first reference date shall be the one corresponding to the quarter in which the issue value of the asset-referenced token is higher than the threshold referred to in [[2023R1114_EN.22#1|Article 22(1)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.
## 3.
The last reference date shall be the one corresponding to the third consecutive quarter in which the issue value of the asset-referenced token is lower than the threshold referred to in [[1114R2023_EN.22#1|Article 22(1)]] of Regulation (EU) No 2023/1114.
