# Article 1 - Definitions


For the purposes of this Regulation, the following definitions apply:
### (a)
‘connected person’ means any of the persons referred to in [[2025R1141_EN.32#1|Article 32(1)]], points (a) to (d) and point (f), of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114;
### (b)
‘group’ means a group as defined in [[2013L0034_EN.2#11|Article 2(11)]] of Directive 2013/34/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council
