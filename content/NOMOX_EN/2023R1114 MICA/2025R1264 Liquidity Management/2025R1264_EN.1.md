# Article 1 - Scope


This Regulation applies to the following issuers of asset referenced tokens and e-money tokens:
### (a)
issuers of significant asset-referenced tokens;
### (b)
electronic money institutions issuing significant e-money tokens;
### (c)
issuers of asset-referenced tokens that are not significant, where required by a competent authority under [[2023R1114_EN.35#4|Article 35(4)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114;
### (d)
electronic money institutions issuing e-money tokens that are not significant, where required by a competent authority under [[2023R1114_EN.58#2|Article 58(2)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.
