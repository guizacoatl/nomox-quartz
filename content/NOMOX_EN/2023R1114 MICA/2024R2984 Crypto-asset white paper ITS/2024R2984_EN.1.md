# Article 1 - Templates for crypto-asset white papers

## 1.
Persons drawing up a crypto-asset white paper referred to in [[2024R2984_EN.6#1|Article 6(1)]], [[2024R2984_EN.19#1|Article 19(1)]] or [[2023R1114_EN.51#1|Article 51(1)]] of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 shall provide the information required by this Regulation in a manner that is non-discriminatory, fair, clear and not misleading, presented in a concise and comprehensible form and shall not omit material information.
## 2.
The templates for the white papers for crypto-assets other than asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens, for asset-referenced tokens and for e-money tokens are set out in the Annex respectively.
