# Article 2 - Legal Entity Identifiers


When using the ISO 17442 Legal Entity Identifier, persons drawing up a crypto-asset white paper referred to in Articles [[2025R0421_EN.6|6]], [[2025R0421_EN.19|Article 19]] or [[2023R1114_EN.51|Article 51 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114]] shall ensure that the identifier is:
### (a)
valid and issued in accordance with the terms of any of the Local Operating Units of the Global Legal Entity Identifier System;
### (b)
included in the Global Legal Entity Identifier database maintained by the Central Operating Unit appointed by the Regulatory Oversight Committee.
