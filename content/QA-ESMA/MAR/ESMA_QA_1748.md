## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 January 2017

How does Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958 apply when the subject of the recommendation is a derivative referencing an index?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-01-2017

[ESMA70-145-111 MAR Q&A, Q&A 8.11]
If a recommendation relates to a derivative referencing an index of financial instruments, the derivative itself should be treated as a financial instrument subject to the requirements of the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958, and not the individual instruments that comprise the index.
