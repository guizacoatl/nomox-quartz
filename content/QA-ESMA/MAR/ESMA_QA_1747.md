## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 January 2017

How does Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/958 apply when the subject of the recommendation relates to several financial instruments independently?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-01-2017

[ESMA70-145-111 MAR Q&A, Q&A 8.10]
Where a recommendation refers to several financial instruments independently, such as part of sectorial research, the requirements would apply to each financial instrument that is the subject of the recommendation.
