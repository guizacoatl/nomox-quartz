## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 March 2023

In which language benchmark statements should be published?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 26-09-2018

ESMA believes that benchmark statements should be published in a language that is accepted by the NCA of the relevant Member State. Administrators are clearly free to publish the benchmark statements also in other additional languages for commercial reasons.
