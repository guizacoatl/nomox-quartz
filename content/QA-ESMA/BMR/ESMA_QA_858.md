## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 March 2021

In Article 51(5) of the BMR, what does “where the benchmark is already used in the Union” mean?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-12-2023

ESMA considers that the meaning of the term “where the benchmark is already used in the Union” in Article 51(5) of the BMR is “where the benchmark is already used in the Union on or before 31 December 2025”.
