## Question

**Submission Date:** 13 September 2012

Are foreign currency bonds of EU Member States also covered by the restrictions on uncovered short sales in sovereign debts?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 13-09-2012

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 10.7]
Yes, the prohibition provision covers sovereign debt securities irrespective of the currency in which they are issued.
