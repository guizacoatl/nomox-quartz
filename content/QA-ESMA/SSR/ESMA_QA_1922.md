## Question

**Submission Date:** 29 January 2013

For the purpose of calculating the net short position in the sovereign debt of a sovereign issuer, is a duration adjustment required to be applied to derivative positions referenced in that sovereign debt?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 29-01-2013

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 7.5]
For the purpose of calculating the net short position in a sovereign debt, ESMA considers that the text of Part 2 of Annex II of the DR only suggests that the positions held through derivative instruments in the sovereign debt of a sovereign issuer (e.g. options, futures, CDS…) should be delta adjusted.
