## Question

**Submission Date:** 29 January 2013

Would the issuance of a Credit Linked Note be subject to the duration adjustment or to the delta adjustment?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 29-01-2013

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 7.6]
Unless a Credit Linked Note is issued directly by a sovereign issuer, it should be treated as any other derivative on a sovereign debt (see Q&A1922).
