## Question

**Submission Date:** 29 January 2013

Can telephone call registrations be considered as a durable medium for the purpose of evidencing the existence of an agreement to borrow or other enforceable claim under Article 5 of the ITS, or of arrangements, confirmations and instructions under Article 6 and 7 of the ITS (Commission Implementing Regulation EU No 827/2012)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 29-01-2013

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 10.3]
Telephone call registrations are considered an adequate durable medium under Article 5, 6 or 7 of the ITS.
