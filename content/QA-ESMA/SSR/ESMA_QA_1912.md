## Question

**Submission Date:** 10 October 2012

Can a short position in sovereign debt be offset against an interest rate swap trade?
(For example, Sell 10 year German Bund and receive Fixed (pay floating) 10 year interest rate swap denominated in Euro)

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 10-10-2012

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 6.7]
No. An interest rate swap is not per se an instrument related to the concerned sovereign debt in the meaning of Article 8(2) of the Regulation unlike derivative instruments on the sovereign debt itself.
