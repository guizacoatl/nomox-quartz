## Question

**Submission Date:** 13 September 2012

what level is the net short position to be calculated in the case of umbrella structures and master feeder structures?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 13-09-2012

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 6.2]
In the case of umbrella structures, the calculation of the net short position must take place at the level of the respective subfunds. In the case of master-feeder structures, it takes place at the level of the respective master fund.
