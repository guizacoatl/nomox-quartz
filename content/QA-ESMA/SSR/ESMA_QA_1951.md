## Question

**Submission Date:** 13 September 2012

Do the restrictions on uncovered sovereign credit default swaps also apply to the protection seller?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 13-09-2012

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 11.1]
No, the restriction on uncovered sovereign credit default swaps applies only to the protection buyer of a CDS.
