## Question

**Submission Date:** 13 September 2012

How will any violations of the provisions of the Regulation be sanctioned?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 13-09-2012

[ESMA70-145-408 SSR Q&A, Q&A 13.1]
Pursuant to Article 41 of the Regulation, rules on penalties and administrative measures shall be established by Member States and notified to ESMA.
In addition, ESMA has to publish a list of the existing penalties and administrative measures applicable in Member States on its website (http://www.esma.europa.eu/page/Short-selling).
