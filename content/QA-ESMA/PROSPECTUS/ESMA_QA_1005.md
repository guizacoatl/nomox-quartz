## Question

**Submission Date:** 12 July 2019

Should the quality of the translations be left entirely to the responsibility of the issuer?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 12-07-2019

[ESMA31-62-1258 Prospectuses Q&A 9.1a]
Yes. ESMA considers that the persons responsible for the prospectus is also responsible for any translation of the approved prospectus.
