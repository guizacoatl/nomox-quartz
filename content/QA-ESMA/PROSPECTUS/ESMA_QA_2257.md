## Question

**Submission Date:** 21 August 2024

How should issuers comply with the requirement to include a hyperlink to new annual or interim financial information that will be incorporated by reference if such information is not yet available at the time of approval of the base prospectus?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 06-06-2025

Answer provided by the European Commission in accordance with Article 16b(5) of the ESMA Regulation
Where applicable, the base prospectus shall contain, in the relevant section, a hyperlink to where the new annual or interim financial information will be published.
