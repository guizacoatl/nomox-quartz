## Question

**Submission Date:** 21 August 2024

How does incorporation of new annual or interim financial information by reference in a base prospectus interact with the general requirement to produce a supplement under Article 23 of the Prospectus Regulation?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 06-06-2025

Answer provided by the European Commission in accordance with Article 16b(5) of the ESMA Regulation
The obligation to produce a supplement under Article 23 of the Prospectus Regulation shall not apply to new annual or interim financial information when it is incorporated by reference under Article 19(1b).
