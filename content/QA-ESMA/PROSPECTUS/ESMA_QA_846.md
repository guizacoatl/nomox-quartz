## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 July 2021

If shares are considered transferable securities, irrespective of the conditions of a shareholders’ agreement restricting the transferability of those shares, should information in relation to the agreement be incorporated in the prospectus?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-07-2021

Answer provided by the European Commission in accordance with Article 16b(5) of the ESMA Regulation
Yes. Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/980 [1] requires, in the prospectus for equity or non-equity securities, information in relation to any restrictions on the transferability of the securities (e.g. Annex 11, item 4.8) and, for equity securities only, information in relation to lock-up agreements of selling securities holders (e.g. Annex 11, item 7.4). In the Prospectus Regulation[2], the definition of securities[3] refers to transferable securities as defined in MiFID II[4]. Where the shares are transferable securities, they fall under the definition of equity securities set out in Article 2(b) of the Prospectus Regulation[5].
