## Question

**Submission Date:** 21 August 2024

Please clarify the scope of ‘financial information’ to which Article 19(1b) of the Prospectus Regulation applies.

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 06-06-2025

Answer provided by the European Commission in accordance with Article 16b(5) of the ESMA Regulation
“Financial information” referred to in Article 19(1b) of Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/2809, shall be understood as including any of the information under Article 19(1)(d), (e), (f), (g), (i) and (j).
