## Question

**Submission Date:** 12 July 2019

Is it mandatory for issuers to set in a base prospectus a fixed amount for the programme?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 12-07-2019

[ESMA31-62-1258 Prospectus Q&A nr. 14.1]
ESMA considers that it is not mandatory to include the amount of the programme in the base prospectus.
