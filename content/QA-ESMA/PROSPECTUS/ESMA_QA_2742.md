## Question

**Submission Date:** 19 December 2025

Do subscription rights fall under the exemptions in Article 1(5)(a) and (ba) of the Prospectus Regulation?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 19-12-2025

Yes, subscription rights fall under these exemptions provided they relate to shares which themselves qualify under these exemptions.
