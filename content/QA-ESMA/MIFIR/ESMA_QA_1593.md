## Question

**Submission Date:** 18 November 2016

Will disaggregation be required at the level of the market operator or at the level of each trading venue?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 18-11-2016

[ESMA 70-872942901-38 MiFID II MiFIR market structures Q&A, Q&A 2.1]
Disaggregation is required at the level of each trading venue for which the market operator or investment firm operating a trading venue has received a specific authorisation under MiFID II.
