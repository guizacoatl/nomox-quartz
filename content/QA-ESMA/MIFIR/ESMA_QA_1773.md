## Question

**Submission Date:** 10 October 2016

Is the recording of telephone conversations and electronic communications a critical or important operational function for the purposes of the outsourcing rules?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 10-10-2016

[ESMA 35-43-349 MiFID II Q&As on Investor protection Ch. 3, question 7]
 Firms may use third-party recording services to meet the new requirement to record telephone conversations and electronic communication. However, ESMA considers that, for the purposes of the outsourcing rules, taping will be considered a critical or important operational function.
