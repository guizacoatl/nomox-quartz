## Question

**Submission Date:** 02 February 2017

How should Field 8 (Request for admission to trading by issuer) be populated?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 02-02-2017

[ESMA 70-1861941480-56 MiFIR data reporting Q&A, Q&A 7.1]
Field 8 (Request for admission to trading by issuer) should be populated with ‘true’ if the request for admission to trading was received from the issuer or the trading venue is aware of the issuer's approval for admission of its financial instrument to trading. Otherwise it should be populated with ‘false’.
