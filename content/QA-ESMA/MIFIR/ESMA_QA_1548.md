## Question

**Submission Date:** 03 April 2017

Do trading venues have to make available their arrangements covering asset classes beyond their current business?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 03-04-2017

[ESMA 70-872942901-35 MiFIR transparency Q&A, Q&A 2.1]
No. Trading venues have to make available their arrangements for all asset classes for which they provide services but not beyond.
