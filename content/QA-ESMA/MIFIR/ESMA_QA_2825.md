## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 April 2026

Is the opt-in regime in Article 22a of MiFIR applicable to bonds and OTC derivatives?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-04-2026

No, the opt-in regime in Article 22a of MiFIR is exclusively applicable to the CTP for shares and ETFs. With regard to bonds and OTC derivatives, all data contributors are required to transmit data to the relevant CTP.
