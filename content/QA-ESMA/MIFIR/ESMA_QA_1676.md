## Question

**Submission Date:** 02 February 2017

Which date and time should be populated by a trading venue in Field 10 (Date of request for admission to trading) in case it receives more than one request for admission to trading of a specific financial instrument on a given day?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 02-02-2017

[ESMA 70-1861941480-56 MiFIR data reporting Q&A, Q&A 3.4]
In case more than one request for admission to trading of a specific financial instrument is received by a trading venue on a given day, Field 10 (Date of request for admission to trading) should be populated with the date and time of the first request for admission to trading.
