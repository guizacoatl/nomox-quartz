## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 April 2026

Are MTFs and OTFs required to generate instrument status messages even when the instrument is already on halt / suspended by a primary exchange?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-04-2026

The instrument status message requires the information of the Market Identifier Code (MIC). Therefore, this information is to be provided per ISIN-MIC-Currency basis.
Hence, MTFs and OTFs should generate the instrument status message whenever they halt / suspend an instrument.
