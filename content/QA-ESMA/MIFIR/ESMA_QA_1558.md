## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2017

How is the concept of “class of bonds” to be understood in respect of the temporary suspension of transparency?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2017

[ESMA 70-872942901-35 MiFIR transparency Q&A, Q&A 4.8]
Since the suspension of transparency under Article 9(4) and 11(2) of MiFIR applies at a class level, with respect to bonds, the classes of bonds defined in Table 2.2 Annex III of RTS 2 should be used.
