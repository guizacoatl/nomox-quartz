## Question

**Submission Date:** 04 February 2017

What date should be populated in Field 15 (Maturity date) for perpetual debt financial instruments?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 04-02-2017

[ESMA 70-1861941480-56 MiFIR data reporting Q&A, Q&A 5.1]
For perpetual debt financial instruments field 15 (Maturity date) should be populated with the value 9999-12-31 (in accordance with the ISO format).
