## Question

**Submission Date:** 09 December 2022

What ISO 3166-1 country code should trading venues and investment firms use to identify stateless natural persons for the purposes of transaction reports?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-03-2023

[ESMA 70-1861941480-56 MiFIR data reporting Q&A, Q&A 24.14]
When identifying stateless natural persons, trading venues and investment firms should use the ISO 3166 code “ZZ”, followed by a CONCAT code generated in accordance with Article 6(4) of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/590 (RTS22).
