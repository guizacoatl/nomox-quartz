## Question

**Submission Date:** 30 October 2017

Do the APM Guidelines require a numeric reconciliation of the APM to “the most reconcilable line item, total or subtotal” presented in the financial statements or is it sufficient to include a qualitative explanation of the items which adjust the financial statement’s figures?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 30-10-2017

[ESMA32-51-370 APM Q16]
Paragraph 28 of the APM Guidelines foresees that “the reconciliation should show how the figure is calculated”. Therefore, issuers should provide a reconciliation in the form of a numeric reconciliation between “the most directly reconcilable line item, total or subtotal” presented in financial statements and the APM used. As it is required in paragraph 26 of the APM Guidelines, the reconciliation should separately identify and explain the material reconciling items.
