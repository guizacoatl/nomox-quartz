## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2019

Does this include a balloon payment on Interest Only underlying exposures?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.3.30]
Yes, this field includes any balloon payment on underlying exposures deemed to be interest-only (corresponding, inter alia, to ‘Bullet’ underlying exposures in the ‘Amortisation Type’ fields in RREL35; CRPL46; AUTL32; ESTL31—as applicable), so long as this payment is paid at maturity of the underlying exposure.
