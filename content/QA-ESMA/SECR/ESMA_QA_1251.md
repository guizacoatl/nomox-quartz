## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 May 2019

What will ESMA publish on its website when it receives an STS notification?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-05-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 4.2]
In addition to the full list of securitisations which have been notified to ESMA in accordance with Article 27 of the Securitisation Regulation as meeting the requirements in of Articles 19 to 22 or Articles 23 to 26 of the Securitisation Regulation, ESMA will publish the individual STS notification templates (https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/esma33-128-585a_template_interim_solution.xlsx) as notified by the reporting entities.
