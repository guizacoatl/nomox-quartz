## Question

**Submission Date:** 26 February 2021

How should these two fields be completed when the relevant tranche(s) do(es) not have an assigned ISIN?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 26-02-2021

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.15.15]
In that case, the unique identifier of the relevant tranche, as entered into SEST3, should be entered in place of the ISIN.
