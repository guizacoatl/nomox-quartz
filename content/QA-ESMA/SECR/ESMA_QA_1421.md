## Question

**Submission Date:** 28 May 2020

Does this field refer only to new arrears after date of transfer to the SSPE and only for underlying exposures which were restructured 1-3 years before transfer?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 28-05-2020

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.13.13]
No. This field refers to exposures that have been restructured by the originator/sponsor at any time AND have at any time been in arrears (either regarding principal or interest payments) since the date of restructuring.
