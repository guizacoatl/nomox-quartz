## Question

**Submission Date:** 17 July 2019

Can a field contain multiple variants of ND options?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 17-07-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.1.11.d]
No. Only one ND-option can be chosen for the same field. If several ND options are relevant, the reporting entity should judge which ND-option is most relevant and enter that one.
