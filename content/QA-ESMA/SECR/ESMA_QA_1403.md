## Question

**Submission Date:** 28 May 2021

Is it correct to provide the first registration date, also in case of used cars? Or should the last registration date be provided for used cars?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 28-05-2021

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.7.4]
Yes, the first date of registration should always be provided in this field also in the case of used cars.
