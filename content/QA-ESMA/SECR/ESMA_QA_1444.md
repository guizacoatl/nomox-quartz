## Question

**Submission Date:** 19 November 2019

If a single underlying exposure goes into default, should the field “IVSS12” by definition be completed with a 'Y'?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 19-11-2021

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.14.22]
No. This field refers exclusively to underlying exposure-related events which trigger changes in the priority of payments or the replacement of any counterparties.
