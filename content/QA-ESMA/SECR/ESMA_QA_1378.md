## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 May 2019

Should all expenses incurred by the special servicer that have not yet been repaid through recoveries be reported in this field?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-05-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.5.1]
No. The field refers only to the cumulative amount of any sums that have been advanced by the Servicer or SSPE and not yet reimbursed by the obligor such as insurance premiums, ground rent and capital expenditures.
