## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2019

How must these fields be completed when the interest rate floor applies to the underlying reference/index rate, and not to the total interest rate?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.3.28]
In such circumstances, the field must be reported by reference to the contractual floor plus margin applicable to the underlying exposure.
