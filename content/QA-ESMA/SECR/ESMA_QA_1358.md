## Question

**Submission Date:** 28 May 2020

Does this number of payments include prepayments?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 28-05-2020

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.3.24]
No, this field refers to the number of regularly-scheduled payments (interest and/or principal) made before securitisation of the underlying exposure, not including any prepayments made.
