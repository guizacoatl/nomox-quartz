## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-01-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.4.4]
In this scenario the most appropriate purpose should be selected. It is not possible to enter in multiple (‘combined’) values across the items in this list field.
