## Question

**Submission Date:** 19 November 2021

As of when will the new “ESMA” templates need to be made available?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 19-11-2021

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.1.1.a]
As of the 23 September 2020, the new “ESMA” templates will need to be made available in accordance with Article 7 of the Securitisation Regulation. For information about how reporting is carried out until ESMA has registered its first Securitisation Repository, please see Q&A 1245.
