## Question

**Submission Date:** 17 July 2019

Among the response options to this field is “Collateralised Loan Obligation (CLOB)”. Does this option indicate that the securitisation is a CLO or that the underlying exposure is a CLO?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 17-07-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.13.3]
This response option means that an underlying exposure is a CLO. This response option is included for completeness, in light of possible underlying exposure types found in ABCP transactions.
