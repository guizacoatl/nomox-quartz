## Question

**Submission Date:** 17 July 2019

How should this field be completed in case the payment terms of a receivable are specified in categories which are not exact (e.g. 30-45 days)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 17-07-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.6.6]
In case the payment terms of a receivable are specified in the range 30-45 days, then the correct value for this field is the latest day before the payment becomes overdue, i.e. 45 days.
