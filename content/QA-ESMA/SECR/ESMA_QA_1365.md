## Question

**Submission Date:** 28 May 2020

With respect to the dates at which the underlying exposure has been restructured, is there a certain time frame for which this assessment is made (e.g. 3 years in line with credit impaired obligors)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 28-05-2020

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.3.31]
There is no time after which this field does not have to populated. If the underlying exposure has been restructured in the past then it must be completed accordingly.
