## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2019

Are there any transitional provisions in place for completing these disclosure templates?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.1.2]
As further explained in section 2.1.2 of ESMA’s CP on disclosure technical standards, the templates must be completed for securitisations that issue any securities from 01/01/2019 onwards (‘new securitisations’), as well as securitisations that issued securities before 01/01/2019 and seek to obtain STS status (‘legacy STS securitisations’).
ABCP programmes that issue securities (i.e. commercial paper) from 01/01/2019, must also complete these templates as required in the RTS on disclosure, with the same considerations applying to ABCP programmes that have issued securities prior to 01/01/2019 but also seek to obtain STS status (‘legacy ABCP programmes’).
