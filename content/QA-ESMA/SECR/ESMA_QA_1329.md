## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 January 2019

How are arrears defined? Is there a standard definition that should be used?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-01-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.2.11]
The Securitisation Regulation does not provide a definition of ‘arrears’. Reporting entities should use the most appropriate regulatory or supervisory definition applicable to them and/or to the underlying exposure.
