## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 January 2019

What is the amount of underlying exposure guaranteed? Does this include the future income pledged by guarantors (in the event of family relations acting as guarantors)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-01-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.4.3]
The amount guaranteed refers to the amount of the underlying exposure that has been guaranteed. This will typically refer to the principal balance of the underlying exposure, although this may also vary with respect to specific underlying exposure terms and conditions.
