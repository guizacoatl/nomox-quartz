## Question

**Submission Date:** 17 July 2019

For synthetic transactions, where the risk transfer method cannot be “true sale”, how should this field be completed?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 17-07-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.14.17]
For any transaction, for which the risk transfer method is not “true sale”, the appropriate response option is ‘N’ (‘No’).
