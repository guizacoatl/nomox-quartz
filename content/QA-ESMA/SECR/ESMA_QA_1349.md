## Question

**Submission Date:** 27 May 2019

How should the Primary Income Verification field be completed where the obligor is a legal person?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-05-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.3.14]
The most appropriate verification option should be entered. For example, if income (in this case, revenue) has been verified, then the ‘VRFD’ (representing ‘Verified’) should be entered. If a credit bureau has been used instead, then ‘SCRG’ (representing ‘Credit Bureau Information or Scoring’) should be entered.
