## Question

**Submission Date:** 28 May 2020

In cases where several ABCP Programmes fund the same ABCP Transaction, how many ‘reporting entities’ should be designated in accordance with Article 7(2)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 28-05-2020

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.1.8.c]
For each ABCP programme, one entity should be designated in accordance with Article 7(2) to make available all the relevant information set out in Q&A 1262 relating to that ABCP programme and all the ABCP transactions within that ABCP programme.
