## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 January 2019

How should this field be completed for leases?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-01-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.7.1]
This field should also be completed for leases, using the ratio of the discounted lease balance (inclusive of all fees and other amounts owed by the obligor) relative to the automobile value at origination (as set out in field AUTL60).
