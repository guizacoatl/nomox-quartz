## Question

**Submission Date:** 17 July 2019

What data should be reported in the fields IVSR3 and IVSR4 in cases where there has been a breach of test or of a trigger?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 17-07-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.14.14]
The data provided in IVSR3 and IVSR4 should not be modified due to a breach of a test or a trigger. Such an event is instead captured by the fields IVSR6 (Actual Value) and IVSR7 (Status).
