## Question

**Submission Date:** 31 January 2019

What is meant by ‘also being managed by the CLO manager’?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 31-01-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.6.1]
This refers to a situation where the CLO manager also manages a participation in the same underlying exposure, and that participation is not securitised.
