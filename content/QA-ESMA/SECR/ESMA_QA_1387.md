## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2021

How must this field be completed where the “sponsor” is a joint venture, or an externally managed fund. Which entity or entities should be named here?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2021

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.5.10]
Where the sponsoring arrangement for this underlying exposure is being provided by a joint venture (i.e. multiple entities), then the lead (coordinating) entity should be entered here. In the context of a fund with an external manager and multiple investors, then the external manager must be entered here.
