## Question

Should interest penalties be included?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-05-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.14.11]
Any penalties that are treated as interest payments should be included in this field. Penalties that are treated as fees should not be included in this field.
