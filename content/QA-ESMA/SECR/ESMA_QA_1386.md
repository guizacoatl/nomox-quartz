## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 November 2019

How should this field be reported where there is no swap contracted for the underlying exposure?

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-11-2019

[ESMA 33-128-563 Securitisation Q&A, Q&A 5.5.9]
In cases where there is no swap, “NOPE” (No Indemnification from obligor) should be reported for this field.
