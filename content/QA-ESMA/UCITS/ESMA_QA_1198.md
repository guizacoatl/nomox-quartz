## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 July 2012

Could UCITS using the VaR approach to calculate global exposure disclose leverage based on the Commitment Approach?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-07-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 5, Q&A 2b]
Yes. However, the leverage should be disclosed based on both the ‘sum of the notionals’ as provided by CESR’s guidelines and the Commitment Approach.
