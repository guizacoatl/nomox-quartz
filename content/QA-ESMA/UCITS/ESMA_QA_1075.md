## Question

**Submission Date:** 12 June 2023

Pursuant to Article 6(2) of the UCITS Directive, are UCITS management companies allowed to manage pension schemes under Directive (EU) 2016/2341?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 13-06-2023

[ESMA34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, Section I, Q&A 8b]
Yes, provided that it is authorised by national legislation implementing the UCITS Directive. The scope of the UCITS license allows UCITS management companies to undertake as core services only the management of UCITS. However, Article 6(3), point (a), of the UCITS Directive provides the possibility for Member States to authorise UCITS management companies to provide, in addition to the management of UCITS, the management of pension funds’ portfolios, in accordance with mandates given by investors on a discretionary, client-by-client basis, where such portfolios include one or more of the instruments listed in Section C of Annex I to Directive 2004/39/EC. Therefore, Member States can authorise UCITS management companies, in addition to the management of UCITS, to manage investment portfolios of pension funds only on a mandate basis, acting as service providers and not as investment managers of the pension funds.
