## Question

**Submission Date:** 15 March 2013

In some jurisdictions, UCITS management companies may also act as securities lending agents. In this case, what information should be provided to investors?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 15-03-2013

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 4b]
First, pursuant to paragraph 28 of the guidelines, it should be disclosed to investors that the UCITS management company acts a securities lending agent. Also, according to paragraph 35 of the guidelines, the annual report of the UCITS should provide investors with details on the amount of fees paid to the UCITS management company that may be deducted from the gross revenues arising from efficient portfolio management techniques.
