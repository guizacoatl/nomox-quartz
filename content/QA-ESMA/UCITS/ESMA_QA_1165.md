## Question

**Submission Date:** 24 March 2014

Do the guidelines on financial indices apply only to index-tracking UCITS?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 24-03-2014

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 7b]
No, the guidelines on financial indices apply to any UCITS investing in financial indices and not only to index-tracking UCITS. This means that the guidelines on ETFs and other UCITS issues take precedence over the guidelines on eligible assets issued by CESR in 2008 (Ref. CESR/07-044b) and that UCITS should not invest even a small amount of their assets in financial indices that do not comply with paragraphs 48 to 61 of the guidelines.
