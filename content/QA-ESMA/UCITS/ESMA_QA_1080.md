## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 September 2012

For a structured UCITS, as defined in Article 36 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 583/2010 that is no longer marketed to the public, should there be an obligation to update the KIID?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-09-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 2, Q&A 1c]
Yes. A structured UCITS, as defined in Article 36 of Commission Regulation (EU) No 583/2010, needs to keep its KIID up to date.
