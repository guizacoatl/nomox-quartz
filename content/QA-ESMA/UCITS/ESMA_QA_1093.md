## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 September 2012

Is it possible to signpost to a glossary?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-09-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 2, Q&A 5a]
Yes. However, as provided by the guide to clear language and layout for the Key Investor Information document (ref. CESR/10-1320), the use of a glossary should not result in too numerous cross-references.
