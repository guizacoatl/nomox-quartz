## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 July 2012

If the UCITS is a self-managed investment company, what information should be provided under the heading ‘details of contact person at the management company’ in Part A of the notification letter?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-07-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 4, Q&A 4]
If the UCITS is a self-managed investment company, the details of the contact person at the self-managed investment company and the relevant contact information should be provided.
