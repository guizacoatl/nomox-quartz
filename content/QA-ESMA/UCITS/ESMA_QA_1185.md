## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 July 2012

Should all the documents referred in to Article 93(2) of Directive 2009/65/EC be transmitted when UCITS send updates of documents to the competent authorities of the host Member States pursuant to Article 32(2) of Directive 2010/42/EU?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-07-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 4, Q&A 2d]
No. Only the documents which have been modified should be transmitted to the competent authority of the host Member State.
