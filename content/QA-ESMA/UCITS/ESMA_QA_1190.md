## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 July 2012

Under Part B of the model notification letter set out in Annex I of Regulation 584/2010, the UCITS’ host Member State may require evidence of payment of notification fees. How should this evidence of payment be provided?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-07-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 4, Q&A 6]
There should be evidence that the notification fee has been transferred e.g. by a scan of the transfer form. The evidence should be attached to the notification as proof of payment.
