## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 October 2016

Can cash collateral received by UCITS in the context of EPM techniques or OTC financial derivative transactions be used by UCITS for clearing obligations under EMIR?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-10-2016

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 6j]
No. Cash collateral received by UCITS can only be placed or invested in the assets listed in paragraph 43(j) of the guidelines.
