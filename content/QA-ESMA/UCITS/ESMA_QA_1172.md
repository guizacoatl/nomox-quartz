## Question

**Submission Date:** 24 March 2014

According to paragraph 59 of the guidelines, UCITS should not invest in financial indices whose methodologies permit retrospective changes to previously published index values (‘backfilling’). Does this provision cover calculation mistakes?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 24-03-2014

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 7i]
No. Calculation mistakes are not covered by paragraph 59 of the guidelines.
