## Question

**Submission Date:** 09 January 2015

What is the scope of application of paragraphs 37, 39 and 40 of the guidelines?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 09-01-2015

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 5c]
Paragraphs 37, 39 and 40 of the guidelines apply to any financial derivative instrument by which UCITS gain exposure to an asset.
