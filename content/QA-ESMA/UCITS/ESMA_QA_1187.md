## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 July 2012

When complying with the obligation of access to documents as required by Article 31(1) of Directive 2010/42/EU, can UCITS use password-protected documents?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-07-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 4, Q&A 3b]
No. The use of password-protected documents by UCITS is not permitted.
