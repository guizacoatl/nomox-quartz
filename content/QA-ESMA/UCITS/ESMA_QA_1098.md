## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 September 2012

Should the name of the investment manager(s) of the UCITS, if any, be disclosed in the KIID?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-09-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 2, Q&A 6]
No. Only the name of the UCITS management company should be disclosed.
