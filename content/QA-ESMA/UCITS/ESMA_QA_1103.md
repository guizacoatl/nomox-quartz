## Question

**Submission Date:** 11 July 2013

Can the prospectus of an index-tracking UCITS mention both replication methodologies (physical and synthetic replication)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 11-07-2013

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 1a]
Yes. If the UCITS intends to use both replication methodologies either at the same time or alternatively, this should be reflected in the prospectus.
