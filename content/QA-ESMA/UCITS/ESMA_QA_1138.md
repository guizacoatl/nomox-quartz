## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 October 2016

When there is transfer of title, can the collateral be held by a custodian that is not the depositary of the UCITS?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-10-2016

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 6e]
Yes, but only if the UCITS’ depositary has delegated the custody of the collateral to a sub-custodian and the depositary remains liable if the collateral is lost by the sub-custodian.
