## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 March 2019

What should be displayed in the bar chart for years when there is no data?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-03-2019

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 2, Q&A 4d]
Pursuant to Article 15, paragraph 3 of Regulation 583/2010, when there is no data available, the year shall be shown as blank with no annotation other than the date.
