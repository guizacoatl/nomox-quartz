## Question

**Submission Date:** 11 July 2013

Do the provisions on Index-tracking UCITS also apply to UCITS ETFs?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 11-07-2013

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 3, Q&A 1b]
Yes, to the extent that the UCITS ETF is tracking an index or indices.
