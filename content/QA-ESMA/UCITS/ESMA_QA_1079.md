## Question

**Submission Date:** 01 September 2012

Should there be an obligation to prepare a KIID for a UCITS that is in liquidation?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 01-09-2012

[ESMA 34-43-392 UCITS Q&A, section 2, Q&A 1b]
When a UCITS is in liquidation there can be no obligation to prepare a KIID as the liquidator may have assumed many of the powers of the UCITS management company.
