## Question

**Submission Date:** 05 July 2024

Are AIFMs permitted to hold client money, taking into account also the wording of Article 6(4)(b)(ii) of the AIFMD?

Will the situation change in light of the legislative amendments introduced following the AIFMD Review (Directive 2024/927/EU)?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 06-01-2025

Answer from the European Commission:
No. 
Article 6(4)(b)(ii) of Directive 2011/61/EU (“AIFMD”) states that an AIFM may be authorized to provide safekeeping services in relation to shares or units of collective investment undertakings but does not permit AIFMs to safekeep clients’ money. Such a service is, therefore, not compatible with Article 6(4)(b)(ii) of AIFMD. 
The situation will not change as a result of the extension of the scope of ancillary services under Article 6(4)(b) of the revised AIFMD (Directive (EU) 2024/927).
