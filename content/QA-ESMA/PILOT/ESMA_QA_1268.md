## Question

**Submission Date:** 02 June 2023

Would a DLT financial instrument traded on a DLT MTF be subject to the book-entry form obligation under Article 3 of CSDR unless it is registered in DLT form with a DLT SS/TSS that has applied for the exemption from that Article?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 02-06-2023

[ESMA70-460-189 - Heading 10 - Question 2]
Answer provided by the European Commission in accordance with article 16b(5) of the ESMA Regulation.
Unless the DLT MTF is authorised as DLT TSS and obtains an exemption from applying Article 3 of CSDR, where a transaction in transferable securities takes place on a DLT MTF, the relevant securities shall be recorded in book-entry form in a CSD.
