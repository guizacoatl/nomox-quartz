## Question

**Submission Date:** 16 December 2022

How should a DLT transaction fee  be considered in the context of populating Field 33 “Price” of RTS 22? Should DLT transaction fees be included in the price related fields?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 16-12-2022

Irrespective of whether an exemption from reporting was granted or not, DLT MTFs should not include DLT transaction fees in the price information, they should not be treated as commissions. Therefore Field 33 “Price” should reflect only the traded price of the transaction.
