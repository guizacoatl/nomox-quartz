## Question

**Submission Date:** 07 July 2017

Should investment firms include positions traded on a trading venue and economically equivalent OTC contracts in position reports under Article 58(2) of MiFID II?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 11-12-2025

This Q&A is deleted as Article 58(2) of MiFID II specifies that investment firms should only include economically equivalent OTC reports in position reports under Article 58(2) of MiFID II.
