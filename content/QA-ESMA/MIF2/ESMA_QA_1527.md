## Question

**Submission Date:** 09 August 2023

Who should submit position reports under Article 58(2) of MiFID II?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 11-12-2025

Only investment firms trading in commodity derivatives or derivatives of emission allowances outside a trading venue (economically equivalent OTC contracts) should submit position reports under Article 58(2) of MiFID II.
