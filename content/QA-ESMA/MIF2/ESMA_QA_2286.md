## Question

**Submission Date:** 18 September 2024

What is the exact scope of the ancillary service, point 5 in annex I, Section B: Investment research and financial analysis or other forms of general recommendations relating to financial instruments?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 26-11-2024

ANSWER:
The activities consisting of investment research and financial analysis or other forms of general recommendations relating to financial instruments are ancillary services listed in Section B(5) of Annex I of Directive 2014/65/EU. Other forms of general recommendations, such as the ones that are not labelled as investment research or do not include a statement indicating that the recommendation is objective and has been prepared in an independent way, include any 7 form of general recommendation about a transaction in a financial instrument or a type of financial instrument, including those falling under the definition of investment recommendations as set out in Article 3(1)(35) of Regulation (EU) No 596/2014. Pursuant to Article 6 of Directive 2014/65/EU, where an entity providing investment services listed in Section A of Annex I of that Directive provides the ancillary services described in Section B of Annex I, its authorisation should also cover those ancillary services, including those set out in Section B (5) of Annex I. However, and without prejudice to any national law requirements applicable to ancillary services, an entity providing only the services referred to in Section B of Annex I of Directive 2014/65/EU does not have to obtain an authorisation pursuant to that Directive, as it cannot be solely granted for the provision of ancillary services.
