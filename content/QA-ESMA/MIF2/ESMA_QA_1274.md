## Question

**Submission Date:** 23 September 2022

Should positions with different maturities for other months’ limits be netted?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 23-09-2022

[ESMA 70-872942901 Commodity derivatives, Position limits, Q&A 4]
Yes. Persons must determine their net position for each commodity derivative for the other months’ limit, as indicated in Article 3 (4-7) of RTS 21a.
They should sum (or net, as appropriate) all individual positions across the curve excluding those positions in the spot month for that commodity derivative.
