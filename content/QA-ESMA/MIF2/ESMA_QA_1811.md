## Question

**Submission Date:** 23 March 2018

What does “hold a retail client account” mean in the context of Article 62(2) of the MiFID II Delegated Regulation?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 23-03-2018

[ESMA 35-43-349 MiFID II Investor protection Q&As Ch. 8, question 11]
The phrase ”hold a retail client account” could be understood as: 

providing the ancillary service of (1) Section B of Annex 2 of MIFID II of safekeeping and administration of financial instruments for the account of retail clients, or; 
holding an account intended for registering client’s transactions on financial instruments (in the context of an investment service provided to a retail client)
