## Question

**Submission Date:** 16 February 2026

Are counterparties immediately relieved from the representativeness obligation, including reporting, when they no longer meet the EUR 6 billion threshold set out under Article 7a(4) of EMIR?

---

## ESMA Answer

**Answer Date:** 27-02-2026

The representativeness obligation, including reporting, ceases to apply as soon as a counterparty no longer meets the EUR 6 billion threshold set out under Article 7a(4) of EMIR. 
However, to enable NCAs to verify compliance, a counterparty must still submit the reporting covering the last period during which it was subject to the representativeness obligation. 
Counterparties must keep monitoring their positions on a continuous basis against the relevant thresholds.
